# Introduction

## Introduction

Roblox Manager is a Windows app for managing Roblox accounts and sessions.

Use one workspace to organize accounts and launch the sessions you need.

### What you can do

Roblox Manager helps you:

* Manage accounts with aliases, groups, and pins.
* Launch games individually or in bulk.
* Check account presence and tile open windows.

### Who it is for

Roblox Manager supports people who regularly use more than one Roblox account.

It reduces repeated account switching and keeps sessions easier to manage.

### Platform support

Roblox Manager supports Windows 10 and Windows 11. macOS and Linux are unsupported.

### Important notes

{% hint style="warning" %}
Never share your `.ROBLOSECURITY` cookie. Roblox updates can affect multi-instance support.
{% endhint %}

Roblox Manager is independent and unaffiliated with Roblox Corporation.
